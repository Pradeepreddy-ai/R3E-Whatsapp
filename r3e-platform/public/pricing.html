Billing Questions

Common questions about pricing

Is there a free trial?+
Yes — every plan includes a full 14-day free trial with access to all features. No credit card is required to start. At the end of the trial, you choose whether to subscribe. Your data is retained if you decide not to continue.
Can I change my plan at any time?+
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades apply at the start of your next billing cycle.
Are WhatsApp messages included in the plan price?+
R3E does not charge per message. You will need your own WhatsApp Business API account through Meta. Standard Meta conversation fees apply based on your region and message volume — these are typically very low.
What happens if I reach my customer limit?+
You will receive a notification when you reach 90% of your customer limit. Your existing campaigns continue running for current customers. To add more customers, simply upgrade your plan — it takes seconds.
How does the annual discount work?+
Paying annually saves you 20% compared to the monthly rate. Starter becomes £39/month (£468/year) and Growth becomes £103/month (£1,236/year). You are billed once per year for the full amount.
How does Enterprise pricing work?+
Enterprise pricing is customised based on your number of locations, customer volume, and required features. Contact our sales team for a tailored quote. Most enterprise agreements include volume discounts, bespoke SLA terms, and a dedicated account manager.
